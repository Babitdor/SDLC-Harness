# Constraints Register — Substation Battery Energy Storage System

Document: CON-BESS-018
Revision: B

C1–C3 are stated in the requirements specification; this register continues from C4.

## Physical & Performance

| ID | Constraint | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C4 | Usable energy capacity | ≥ 2 MWh | Within the 10–95 % SOC window (C8), so nameplate ≥ 2.35 MWh |
| C5 | Continuous power output | ≥ 1 MW | At point of common coupling |
| C6 | Round-trip efficiency | ≥ 85 % | AC-to-AC, including auxiliaries |
| C7 | Operating temperature | 15 – 35 °C | Per-module cell temperature |
| C8 | State of charge window | 10 – 95 % | SOC operating band |
| C9 | Voltage regulation | ±5 % of nominal | At point of connection; ANSI C84.1 Range A |
| C10 | Frequency response time | ≤ 500 ms | To reach commanded set-point |
| C11 | Over-current clearing time | ≤ 100 ms | Protection trip time |

## Budget & Timeline

| ID | Constraint | Value |
|---|---|---|
| C12 | Maximum capital expenditure | $2.5 M (equipment + installation) |
| C13 | Project timeline | 18 months (kick-off to commercial operation) |
| C14 | Warranty period | 10 years or 4 000 cycles (whichever first) |

## Safety & Environmental

Cell chemistry: lithium iron phosphate (LFP).

| ID | Constraint |
|---|---|
| C15 | The installation must comply with NFPA 855 setback and ventilation requirements. |
| C16 | Battery modules must be housed in a dedicated enclosure with fire-rated walls (≥ 2 h rating). |
| C17 | A gas-detection and exhaust system must activate before thermal runaway conditions are reached. |
| C18 | Where free-flowing liquid is present (for example a liquid-cooled thermal system), containment shall capture 110 % of that liquid volume. LFP modules contain no free-flowing electrolyte. |
